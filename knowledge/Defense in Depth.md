**Defense in depth** places multiple defensive layers in front of each risk, assuming every single defense will fail at some point (see [[Security Risk#Residual risk|residual risk]]). Each layer reduces risk.

## The "egg" anti-pattern
Perimeter-only security treats the system as an egg: attacks come from outside the shell. Once an attacker breaches the edge, they can attack the inside however they please. Layers behind the perimeter limit what a breach can reach.
