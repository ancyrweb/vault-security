**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is [[Authentication]] that combines multiple kinds of evidence about the user.

## Factors
- **Something you know**: password
- **Something you have**: digital security key, mobile phone, one-time PINs
- **Something you are**: fingerprint, face scan
- **Where you are**
