**Security principles** guide where and how to defend a system. Most come back to managing [[Security Risk]].

- [[Security Risk]]: likelihood × impact, plus the residual risk that always remains
  - [[Common Vulnerability Scoring System|CVSS]]: the standard 0–10 severity score for vulnerabilities
- [[Attack Surface Minimization]]: less code and less data means less risk
- [[Defense in Depth]]: several layers per risk, because each one will fail
- [[Threat Modeling]]: find the likely attacks, then direct defenses at them
